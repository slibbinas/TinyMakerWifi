Resins tested for TinyMaker

The TinyMaker has a colour LCD and a 40 x 31 mm plate, so the numbers on a resin bottle rarely fit it. Everything below was printed on the machine itself - the exposure listed is the one that worked, not the one on the label.

Mockup. Layout study for the public resin catalogue. Photographs are drawings, and one entry is a synthetic example used to show what a fully tested resin looks like. Printed numbers for Sunlu Toughness and Anycubic Water-Washable are real - they come from the profiles the printer already ships.

Tested and published

Each of these ran through the same four steps: exposure strip, standard coupon, a simple model, a detailed one. The profile is installed on the printer in one tap from the dashboard.
